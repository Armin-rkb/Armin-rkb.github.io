03 — About

Howdy! I'm Armin.

I'm a game developer with about 1.5 years at Paladin Studios, and a graduate of Breda University of Applied Sciences. Making games has been a dream since I was a kid taking games apart, modding them and building custom maps to see how they tick.

I'm always looking to grow. Giving and getting feedback matters a lot to me, and I like reviewing code with my colleagues so we all catch more. I aim for code that is loosely coupled and easy to read, and I work comfortably in English or Dutch.

Group projects are my favourite: a room full of friendly, talented people who care as much as I do. Outside of work I game a lot, and I like trying new things: making clothes, photography, video, music and drawing.
